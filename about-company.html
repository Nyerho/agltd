About Us

Indigenous expertise, strong partnerships, and premium value delivery.

Aquilagalaxy Solutions is a multidisciplinary Nigerian company serving the Energy industry and related sectors.

WHO WE ARE

A tech-savvy indigenous firm delivering engineering, construction, project management, manpower, and operational support.

Company Profile

From concept to decommissioning, we support clients with competence and commitment.

AQUILAGALAXY SOLUTIONS is a privately owned indigenous, tech-savvy multidisciplinary firm, with competences in the Energy Industry, Geoscience, Civil Engineering and Construction, Project Management, Security Services, Labour Supply and Head Hunting from subsequent-to-surface and concept, to decommissioning.

Our technical expertise and strong partnership traits provide clients and customers what they need to succeed, combined with our history of timely delivery and sticking to standard even in the most challenging environments. Our approach delivers superior service delivery to our clients and returns for our shareholders.

We are a global provider of products, systems, and services to industries, especially Energy, and to government agencies, parastatals, and individuals. We create solutions to unlock excellent service delivery, which are safe and sustainable for future generations.

Mission Statement

To provide high quality services in Engineering, Construction, Environmental processes and related services in a safe, efficient, cost-effective and environmentally friendly manner.

Our Mission

To sustainably deliver profitable, diverse engineering solutions through operational excellence and effective partnership.

Our Vision

To build a world class indigenous independent company and remain a reference point for true local content development.

Values

Integrity and Exceptional Solutions.

Corporate Responsibility

Our corporate responsibility at AQUILAGALAXY SOLUTIONS is about making good, sustainable business decisions to benefit the company, our stakeholders and our operational environment.

Strategic Objectives

  • Grow our core business services to maintain leadership in energy service delivery and gas sector development.
  • Develop and exploit local engineering capabilities across safety, environmental, marine logistics, project, and quality management.
  • Expand capability into neighbouring markets with a full range of services.
  • Develop employees into a professional and fully committed team in the Energy industry.

Management Commitment

The company is totally committed to the client's needs, provision of fast, responsive and trouble-free services while utilizing experienced and professional personnel that pay adequate attention to quality and details with proven Nigerian expertise.

The principals of AQUILAGALAXY SOLUTIONS have a broad and specialized background in the Energy industry spanning several years and are respected for their partnership agreements, maintenance services, and knowledge of engineering, procurement, safety and environmental delivery.

We are a growing niche in the industry, welcoming new innovations and trends while maintaining strong local competence.
