Electrical & Instrumentation

AQUILAGALAXY SOLUTIONS is an experienced leader in electrical construction and the installation of industrial instrumentation and control systems. Our experience spans power systems, combined cycle plant construction, petrochemical, food processing, flow stations and heavy industrial plants.

From ground-up construction, our management and technical teams also perform yearly maintenance and expansion projects, critical path turnarounds, emergency response and staff augmentation services.

  • System installation and maintenance
  • Industrial electrification and transmission line support
  • Calibration, preventive maintenance, loop check, commissioning, and start-up support
  • Industrial fibre-optic and copper cabling for voice, data, process control, and video systems

Human Capacity Development

To meet the ever-growing shortage of skilled manpower in the oil and gas industry, especially in Nigeria, AQUILAGALAXY SOLUTIONS has put together seminars and bespoke training courses for operators, service companies, and government petroleum ministries.

These courses are designed and delivered by experienced specialists and industry experts in co-operation with leading Nigerian universities and research institutes.

Training Topics

  • Introduction to Petroleum Engineering and modern offshore technologies
  • Pipelines, risers and flowlines, asset integrity, risk and reliability
  • Onshore refineries and power plants
  • Inspection, NDT, advanced NDT, rope access, and intelligent pigging
  • Subsea engineering, intervention, subsea life field integrity management, and project development
  • Petroleum economics, oil and gas law
