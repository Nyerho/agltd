Who We Are

WHO WE ARE

AQUILAGALAXY SOLUTIONS is a privately owned indigenous, tech-savvy multidisciplinary firm, with competences in the Energy Industry, Geoscience, Civil Engineering and Construction, Project Management, Security Services, Labour Supply and Head Hunting — from subsequent-to-surface and concept, to decommissioning.

Our technical expertise and strong partnership traits provide clients and customers what they need to succeed, combined with our history of timely delivery and sticking to standard even in the most challenging environments. Our approach delivers superior service delivery to our clients and returns for our shareholders.

We are a global provider of products, systems, and services to industries (especially Energy) and their government agencies/parastatals and individuals. We create solutions to unlock excellent service delivery, which are safe and sustainable for future generations.
